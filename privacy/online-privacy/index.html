Marble Hopper Online Privacy Policy

Nicked Studios · Effective November 13, 2026

This policy explains how Marble Hopper Online (the "game"), made by Nicked Studios ("we", "us"), handles your information. It covers the game on Android and in web browsers. (The separate offline game, Marble Hopper, has its own policy.)

The short version: to let you play online, keep your wins, and have a friends list, the game uses a player account with Unity's online services. Other players can see your player name, your rankings, and (if you're friends) whether you're online. We don't ask for your real name, email address, location or contacts, we don't show ads, and we never sell your information. You can delete your account at any time, in the game or by email.

Who can play online

Online play, accounts, leaderboards and friends are for people aged 13 and older. They are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Information the game uses

Your account

  • Player ID. When the game starts, it signs you in to Unity's sign-in service as a guest. This creates a random player ID. A sign-in token is kept on your device so you stay signed in.
  • Username and password (only if you choose "Save account"). These let you sign in on other devices. Passwords are handled by Unity's sign-in service; we never see them.
  • Player name. A random name with a number (for example "Bright Fox#4821"), or your username once you save your account. Other players see it in games, on leaderboards and in friends lists.

Online games

  • Waiting rooms and matchmaking: your player name and ID, which board and game type you chose, and the join code of the game.
  • Connections: to connect players, Unity's relay service handles your device's network connection, including its IP address, while you play. The die rolls and moves of each game are sent between the players' devices.
  • Game results for ranked games: a game ID, the board, the game type, the players' IDs and who won. These are kept to count wins and to check that results are genuine.

Leaderboards

For ranked games we keep your number of wins on each board's monthly leaderboards and the all-time leaderboards. Leaderboards are public to everyone who plays: they show your player name, wins and rank. Each month's final standings are kept as an archive.

Achievements

The achievements you've earned, your points, and your win counts (per board and game size) are saved with your account, so they follow you between devices. Ranked wins are counted by Unity's online services when a ranked game ends; other wins and dice achievements are recorded by the game.

Friends

  • Your friends list, friend requests you send and receive, and players you block.
  • Your online status (for example "Online", "In a game", or when you were last online). Only your friends can see it.
  • Game invites you send to friends. An invite contains your player name, the board and the game's join code.

Stored on your device

The game saves settings on your device, such as volume, chosen board, marble colour, die style and rules language. For online play it also keeps your sign-in token, a short list of players you've recently played with, game results waiting to be sent, and details needed to rejoin a game after a dropped connection.

Technical information

Unity's online services may record technical information, such as device type, operating system, IP address and error logs, to run and secure their services. We have turned off Unity's usage statistics in the game.

What we don't collect

We don't ask for your real name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, photos or payment details. The game has no advertising and currently has no in-app purchases.

How we use it

  • To run online play: sign-in, matchmaking, waiting rooms, connecting players and keeping games in step, including reconnecting after a dropped connection.
  • To record wins, show leaderboards and track achievements, and to stop false results being counted.
  • To provide your friends list, online status and game invites.
  • To fix problems and keep the game secure.

Who it's shared with

  • Unity Technologies runs the online services the game uses (sign-in, matchmaking, relay, leaderboards, friends and cloud data). They process this information on our behalf, under Unity's Privacy Policy. Their servers may be in a different country from yours.
  • Other players see your player name in games, waiting rooms and leaderboards. Your friends also see your online status and invites you send them.
  • Google Play distributes the Android game and may collect information when you download or update apps, under Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Discord: the "Join Our Discord" button opens our Discord community. Anything you share there is covered by Discord's Privacy Policy. Joining is optional.
  • If the law requires it, we may disclose information to comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell or rent your personal information, and we don't share it for advertising.

How long it's kept

Your account information, friends, wins and achievements are kept for as long as your account exists. Game result records are kept so leaderboards stay accurate. Monthly leaderboards reset on the 1st of each month, and past months are archived. When you delete your account, the information linked to it is deleted as described below.

Deleting your account

In the game: open Play Online, tap Delete account, then tap it again to confirm. Your account is deleted straight away, and the game starts again with a new guest account.

Without the game: email nickedgamestudios@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Marble Hopper Online account", including your username or your player name with its number (for example "Bright Fox#4821"). We will delete your account within 30 days and email you to confirm.

Deleting removes your account (player ID, username, password and player name), your friends list, requests and blocks, and your entries on the current leaderboards, and your achievement progress. Records of past ranked games keep only the random player ID that belonged to the account, which no longer links to you once the account is deleted. Archived standings from past months may still show your old player name. Information on your device is removed by deleting the account in the game or by uninstalling the game (or clearing site data in your browser).

Security

Information is sent over encrypted connections and stored by Unity's online services. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a password you don't use anywhere else.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the game uses information, for example by adding in-app purchases, we will update this policy before that update is released and change the date at the top of this page.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your information? Contact Nicked Studios at nickedgamestudios@gmail.com.
