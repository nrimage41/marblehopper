Marble Hopper Privacy Policy

Nicked Studios · Effective November 11, 2026

This policy explains how Marble Hopper (the "game"), made by Nicked Studios ("we"), handles your information. The short version: the game does not collect, store on our servers, or share any personal information.

Information we collect

None. Marble Hopper has no accounts, sign-in, advertising, analytics, or in-app purchases, and it does not ask for your name, email address, contacts, location, photos, or any other personal or sensitive information. The game does not need an internet connection to play.

Information stored on your device

The game saves a few preferences on your own device so they are remembered between games, such as your music and sound-effect volume, your chosen board, marble colour, and die style. This information never leaves your device and is not sent to us or anyone else. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

Third-party services

The game is built with the Unity game engine. We have turned off Unity's usage statistics; any basic technical information the engine itself may send (such as device model and operating system version) is anonymous, is not used by us, and is handled under Unity's Privacy Policy.

The game is distributed through Google Play. Google Play may collect information when you download or update apps, as described in Google's Privacy Policy. We do not receive personal information about you from Google.

The start menu has a "Join Our Discord" button. Tapping it opens our Discord community in your browser or the Discord app. Anything you share on Discord is covered by Discord's Privacy Policy, not this one. Joining is optional and not needed to play.

Children

Marble Hopper is a family board game suitable for all ages. Because the game collects no personal information, it does not collect personal information from children.

Sharing and selling

We do not sell, rent, or share any personal information, because we do not collect any.

Changes to this policy

If a future update adds features that use personal information (for example, online play, player accounts, or in-app purchases), we will update this policy before that update is released and change the date at the top of this page.
